OUR LODGE

A Comfortable Place to Call Home During Your Island Stay

Moffat Bay Lodge is designed for visitors who want comfortable lodging and easy access to local island activities. Our goal is to keep the travel experience simple, from learning about the lodge to choosing a room and making a reservation.

Guests can explore the lodge and surrounding attractions before creating an account or booking a room. This makes it easy to compare options and decide whether Moffat Bay is the right destination for their trip.
